Privacy Policy

Kyu, the desktop companion by GetKyu · Last updated: August 21, 2026

The short version: Kyu runs entirely on your computer. We operate no server, require no account, and collect no analytics, telemetry, or crash reports. What Kyu learns about you is stored on your device and nowhere else. The app talks to the internet only for the specific purposes listed below — most of them only if you turn them on.

1. What we don't do

Kyu has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend server operated by us. We do not collect usage analytics, telemetry, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or behavioral data. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information — we never receive it in the first place. Kyu does not read your screen, your keystrokes, your browsing history, or your files, except for the specific items you hand to it yourself (for example, a file you drop on Kyu or an image you paste into chat).

2. What stays on your device

Everything Kyu knows lives in local storage on your computer: the name you give Kyu, chat history, Kyu's diary, your routine rhythm, preferences and wardrobe, things you asked Kyu to remember, and streak records. API keys and mail credentials are stored in a local file readable only by your user account (file permission 600); they are excluded from Kyu's export file by design. Deleting the app's data (or using the in-app reset) removes this information. The export/import feature ("carry") produces a local file under your control — it is never uploaded by us.

4. Third-party services you choose

When you bring your own AI key, connect mail, or attach an MCP tool server, your data flows directly from your computer to that service under your agreement with them. We are not a party to that traffic, we cannot see it, and we encourage you to review those providers' privacy policies.

5. Children

Kyu is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to use it. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because the app has no collection mechanism.

6. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to hand over, correct, or erase on our side — your data is already in your hands. You can export it (Settings → export), delete it (in-app reset or removing the app's data folder), and inspect what the app stores at any time. If you have questions about privacy rights under laws such as the CCPA or GDPR, the honest summary is: we have no records of you.

7. Purchases (when available)

The app currently contains no payment functionality. If we later sell licenses or upgrades through our website or Steam, payment will be processed by the store or a merchant-of-record payment provider; we will not receive or store your card details. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

8. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date. Because the app makes no silent data collection, changes here will track visible product changes.
